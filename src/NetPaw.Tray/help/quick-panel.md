# Quick panel

Open with the panel hotkey (default `Ctrl+Alt+N`), a left-click on the tray paw, or by starting `NetPaw.exe` again.

## Typing
- Letters: fuzzy-match your **profiles**, then **repository profiles**, then **device presets**.
- An IPv4 address: switches to *Reach mode* and shows what NetPaw would do.
- `settings`, `edit`, `dhcp`, `clear`: shortcuts to those actions.

## Keys
- `Enter` — apply the highlighted row. The panel stays open and shows progress; it closes itself on success.
- `Ctrl+D` — DHCP on the work adapter.
- `Ctrl+E` — open the editor on the highlighted profile (a repository profile is copied into your own profiles first).
- `Ctrl+R` — in reach mode: replace the primary address instead of adding a secondary.
- `Esc` — close. `F1` — this help.

## Badges
- **current** — the adapter already runs this profile.
- **managed** — deployed by your organisation; apply or copy, never edit.
- **community** / other names — the repository the entry came from.

## Header
The first line shows the work adapter: link dot, name, addresses, gateway, link speed, and how many temporary addresses NetPaw added. Hover for MAC and DNS.
